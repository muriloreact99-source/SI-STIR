# DESIGN.md: SI STIR
## 1. Identity
- Product name: SI STIR. Never write "SI SETIR" or "SI STIR HEBAT".
- Full name: Sistem Informasi Supervisi Terintegrasi Ruang Guru, Ruang Murid, Ruang Orang Tua.
- School: SDN 03 Wonosari, NPSN 40500185, Jln. Lingkar, Makmur, Wonosari, Kabupaten Boalemo, Gorontalo 96262.
- Principal: Ibu Khoyum Supardi, S.Pd.SD, M.Pd.
- Small label on every screen: "Tumbuh Bersama".
- Mood: fresh, tidy, friendly. Playful for children, calm for adults. Gen Z feel but professional. Not a grey government portal.
- All interface text is in Bahasa Indonesia.
## 2. Color (strict, use only these)
- Mint #8AD6D1: role cards, soft backgrounds.
- Teal #359FA0: hero band and structural elements.
- Cream #FFF0C5: page background and small labels.
- Orange #FF8C52: action buttons and selected states.
- Deep teal #12383A: all text and all borders.
- Surface #FFFAE6 for cards on cream. Field #FFFFFF for inputs.
- Status chips (background / text):
  Draf and Belum dikerjakan: #EADFB8 / #4B4524.
  Diajukan and Sudah dikirim: #CFEFEC / #12383A.
  Disetujui and Sudah diperiksa: #CDEFD0 / #174D1F.
  Perlu revisi and Perlu diperbaiki: #FFD9C2 / #7A2E00.
- Contrast: never put white or cream body text on teal. Text on orange is deep teal. Text on mint is deep teal.
- Dark mode (automatic): background #0E2A2C, surface #163A3D, mint cards #1F5254, text #FFF0C5, borders #8AD6D1, shadow #04171A. Orange and teal stay the same.
- Signature motif: a thin band of four equal stripes (mint, teal, cream, orange) under the hero.
## 3. Typography
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans only. Emoji are the only decoration.
- H1 weight 800, 36 to 48 px. H2 weight 800, 28 to 32 px. H3 weight 800, 20 px.
- Body weight 500, minimum 16 px, line height 1.5. Never smaller than 16 px for body text.
- Buttons weight 700, 16 px. Tags and labels weight 800, 13 px, uppercase, letter spacing 0.06em.
- Keep lines under 65 characters wide.
## 4. Shape and elevation
- Cards: radius 24 px (large cards 28 px), 2 px deep teal border, hard offset shadow 5 px 5 px 0 deep teal. No blur shadows, no gradients, no glass effects.
- Buttons: pill shape, minimum height 48 px, 2 px border, shadow 3 px 3 px 0. Pressed state moves 3 px down and right and removes the shadow.
- Inputs: radius 16 px, 2 px border, minimum height 48 px.
- Chips: pill, 2 px border, 14 px bold text.
- Emoji stickers on cards: 32 to 36 px (40 px in Ruang Murid).
## 5. Spacing and layout
- Base unit 8 px. Scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64.
- Page container max width 1120 px, side gutter at least 16 px.
- Mobile 360 to 428 px: one column, bottom navigation, large touch targets, sticky primary action when a form is open.
- Tablet 768 px: two columns, slim side rail.
- Desktop 1280 px and up: left sidebar inside rooms, card grids of 3 to 4 columns.
- No horizontal scrolling at any width. Tables turn into stacked card lists on mobile.
## 6. Navigation (same on every screen)
- Header on web: logo mark with "SI STIR" and the small label "Tumbuh Bersama", menu Beranda, Murid, Orang Tua, Guru, Kepala Sekolah, Bantuan, and a primary button "Masuk". When logged in the button becomes "Keluar".
- Header on mobile: logo and a "Masuk" button. Bottom navigation with 5 items: Beranda, Murid, Orang Tua, Guru, Kepala Sekolah. Icon plus label, active item filled deep teal with cream text.
- Footer: "SI STIR, tumbuh bersama setiap hari." then "SDN 03 Wonosari | NPSN 40500185", the address, and "Kontak [isi nomor atau email sekolah]".
- Rooms are role locked. If the wrong role opens a room, show a gate card: "Ruang ini perlu akun" and a button "Masuk ke SI STIR". The principal can open every room.
## 7. Components
- Button primary (orange), secondary (surface with border), small variant 44 px. States: default, hover, pressed, disabled (55 percent opacity), focus (3 px orange outline, 3 px offset).
- Role card: mint or surface, emoji sticker, H3, one short line, primary button.
- Status chip: see color section. Always show the exact status words.
- Checklist row (jurnal): minimum 56 px tall, large square checkbox, bold label with helper line. Selected row turns green tint with an orange check.
- Progress bar: 16 px tall pill, orange fill, text under it such as "3 dari 7 selesai. Sedikit lagi!".
- Tabs: pill tabs, active tab filled deep teal with cream text. A small orange count badge is allowed.
- Message bubbles for Ruang Penghubung: parent message on surface, teacher reply tinted info and indented.
- Toast: deep teal pill, cream bold text, bottom center, 3 seconds.
- Empty state: dashed border box, one friendly sentence, one next action.
- Form: label above field, helper text optional, inline error text "Kolom ini belum diisi.", submit button with a verb label.
- Archive toolbar: search field plus year select plus button "Unggah Dokumen".
- FAQ accordion: card with plus and minus icon, 52 px tall summary.
- Loading line: "Sebentar ya, sedang dibuka."
## 8. Room personalities
- Ruang Murid: most cheerful. Mint hero card, 40 px stickers, 56 px touch targets, big progress bar.
- Ruang Orang Tua: calm and read first. Four summary cards on top, then reflection form and message thread.
- Ruang Guru and Ruang Kepala Sekolah: calmer and tidier. Cream background, plain surface cards, fewer stickers, denser lists, tabs for sections.
## 9. Writing rules (apply to every visible word)
1. Pronoun: "kamu" for murid. "Anda" for orang tua, guru, and kepala sekolah.
2. All teachers are women. Always write "Ibu" before a teacher or principal name.
3. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
4. Headlines are relaxed but polite.
5. NO hyphens and NO long dashes in any visible text.
6. Avoid the pattern "from A to B", the pattern "not only A but also B", and strings of three adjectives.
7. Name real things (absen, jurnal, tugas). Avoid big words such as revolusi or transformasi.
8. Button labels start with a verb: Kumpulkan Tugas, Kirim Refleksi, Tambah Karya, Aku Hadir.
9. Never use lorem ipsum. Use the sample data below.
